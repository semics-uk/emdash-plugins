**Is this a qualified or advanced signature?** No. It makes simple electronic signatures. Ask your lawyer
whether that suits your documents.

**How long is data kept?** Personal data 12 months and signed records 7 years after an envelope closes, by
default. The signed PDF and its certificate keep the signer's name and IP address (and a minor's name, when a
guardian signed) until the signed-records period ends.

**Can a signer's data be deleted on request?** Yes: **Delete personal data** on the Envelopes page. The signed
record keeps its own period.

**No email is sent.** Set the site URL and an email provider.

**Several signers, codes, reminders, PDFs?** E-sign Pro.

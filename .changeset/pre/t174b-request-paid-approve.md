A booking request that Events Pro charged in full when the buyer asked (T174b) can now be confirmed with **Confirm time** on the Bookings page: Free's approve refuses only a payment whose card is merely held, so a paid request is confirmed with its time and nothing more is charged. When such a request is declined or expires, the buyer's email says their payment has been refunded in full, and the expiry email still goes if the refund came first.
